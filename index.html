My name is Max Hojatzadeh, and I’m a landscape architect with over 23 years of experience in landscape design and construction. I started my academic journey in Iran, where I earned a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture. After moving to Australia, I expanded my expertise by completing a Diploma in Landscape Design and a Landscape Construction course at TAFE NSW. I later graduated with a degree in Landscape Architecture from the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), combining my background in agriculture with a formal education in design and construction.

With more than two decades in the field, I’ve developed a deep appreciation for the technical and creative sides of landscaping. I see landscape design as an art form – each project is an opportunity to craft a space that seamlessly integrates nature with human design.
